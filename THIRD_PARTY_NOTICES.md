This repository publishes the core's source, the Quartus project and the release artefacts. The development tooling, verification suite, pinned reference trees and internal documentation are kept out of the published tree; the notes below therefore cite upstream projects rather than local copies.

* Template_MiSTer platform and `emu` example, in `sys/` and `Arcade-Appoooh.sv`: GPL-2.0-or-later file headers, including Sorgelig copyright notices. Upstream: https://github.com/MiSTer-devel/Template_MiSTer. Full GPLv2 text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html. The tracked `sys/` tree carries small recorded language-correctness and explicit-input fixes against upstream; every changed file keeps its original header. Generated Intel/Altera IP under `sys/` and `rtl/pll*` includes its own file-specific terms.
* TV80 Z80 core, in `rtl/vendor/tv80/` with corrected files in `rtl/derived/`: Guy Hutchison, copyright 2004, MIT-style permission notice in every CPU file; based on Daniel Wallner's T80. Upstream: https://github.com/hutch31/tv80. Only `tv80s` and its dependency closure is compiled. The derived `tv80_core.v` and `tv80_mcode.v` correct Mode 0 RET and NMI timing and the `LD A,R` flag path; the original files are retained unmodified alongside them.
* JT89, in `rtl/vendor/jt89/`: Jose Tejada Gomez, GPL-3.0-or-later, with the upstream LICENSE and per-file notices retained in that directory. Upstream: https://github.com/jotego/jt89. `rtl/sn76496_jt89.sv` is a separately named SN76496 adaptation that keeps the donor's register-decoding structure and replaces the variant-dependent oscillator, defaults, noise and amplitude behaviour. Upstream JT89 is not represented as a verified SN76496 drop-in.
* JT5205, in `rtl/vendor/jt5205/`: Jose Tejada Gomez, GPL-3.0-or-later, with the upstream LICENSE and notices retained in that directory. Upstream: https://github.com/jotego/jt5205. The derived decoder in `rtl/derived/jt5205_adpcm.v` makes the reset comparisons signed; the original file is retained unmodified. Timing and decoder execute as synthesizable HDL with interpolation disabled.
* MAME is an external behavioural reference. The Appoooh driver is copyright Uki; the SN76489A/SN76496 and MSM5205 sound references are copyright Nicola Salmoria and Aaron Giles respectively; all BSD-3-Clause. The Sega 315-5179 decryption tables in `rtl/segacrp2_315_5179.sv` are translated from `src/devices/machine/segacrp2_device.cpp`, copyright Nicola Salmoria and David Haywood, BSD-3-Clause. Upstream source and license: https://github.com/mamedev/mame. Appoooh board details were checked against `src/mame/sanritsu/appoooh.cpp`; PSG variant behavior was checked against `src/devices/sound/sn76496.cpp`.
